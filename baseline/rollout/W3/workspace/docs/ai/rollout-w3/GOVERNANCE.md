# Project Governance

Governance version: W3-1

## Authority and precedence

Applicable law and stronger security controls take precedence. Explicit user decisions retain authority over product intent unless higher authority conflicts.

## Principles

### GOV-001 - Preserve the bounded request

Classification: MUST
Authority: rollout owner
Rationale: W3 validates only a bounded workflow record.

## Amendment process

Amendments require a version, authority, rationale, date, and affected principle IDs.

## Authorized exceptions

No exceptions are active.
