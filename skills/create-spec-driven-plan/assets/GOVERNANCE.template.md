## Authority and precedence

1. Applicable law and binding regulatory obligations cannot be waived by this governance record.
2. Stronger security controls take precedence over product convenience, delivery speed, and governance exceptions.
3. Explicit user decisions retain authority over product intent unless they conflict with applicable law or stronger security controls.
4. This governance governs planning decisions only; it does not silently replace user decisions.
5. Unresolved conflicts between these sources require escalation to the authorized decision maker.
